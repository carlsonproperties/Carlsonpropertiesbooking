Test these searches to see who ranks:


Then search for your target keywords and note who appears:

**Action Steps:**
1. Open an incognito browser window
2. Search each keyword
3. Create a spreadsheet with columns:
   - Keyword
   - Your Rank Position
   - Competitor 1 Position
   - Competitor 2 Position
   - Competitor 3 Position

#### **B. Ubersuggest** (Free - 3 searches/day)
https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/

**How to Use:**
1. Enter competitor domain: `thepillarstaupo.com`
2. Click "Search"
3. Go to "Top Pages" → See their best-performing content
4. Go to "Keywords" → See what keywords they rank for
5. Export the data (or screenshot it)

**What to Look For:**
- Keywords with high traffic and low difficulty
- Page titles that rank well
- Content topics they're targeting

#### **C. Google Search Console** (Free - Your Own Data)
https://search.google.com/search-console

**Compare Your Performance:**
1. Go to Performance → Search Results
2. Export your top keywords
3. Compare against competitor keywords you found
4. Identify gaps where competitors rank but you don't

### Paid Tools (More Comprehensive):

#### **A. Ahrefs** (~$99/month) - Industry Standard
https://ahrefs.com

**Best Features:**
- **Site Explorer:** Enter competitor URL → See ALL their ranking keywords
- **Content Gap:** Compare up to 5 competitors against your site
- **Top Pages:** See their highest-traffic pages
- **Keyword Difficulty:** Assess which keywords you can realistically target

**Step-by-Step:**
```
1. Site Explorer → Enter: thepillarstaupo.com
2. Organic Keywords → Export to CSV
3. Filter by: Position 1-10, Traffic > 10
4. Repeat for each competitor
5. Combine data in Google Sheets
```

#### **B. SEMrush** (~$119/month)
https://semrush.com

**Best Features:**
- **Organic Research:** See competitor keyword rankings
- **Keyword Gap Tool:** Compare 5 domains simultaneously
- **Position Tracking:** Monitor competitor rank changes daily
- **Traffic Analytics:** Estimate competitor website traffic

**How to Find Keyword Gaps:**
```
1. Keyword Gap Tool
2. Enter your domain: carlsonproperties.co.nz
3. Add up to 4 competitors
4. Click "Compare"
5. Filter: "Missing" or "Weak" keywords
6. Export high-opportunity keywords
```

#### **C. SpyFu** (~$39/month) - Budget Option
https://spyfu.com

**Best Features:**
- See competitor's paid keywords (Google Ads)
- Historical ranking data
- Cheaper alternative to Ahrefs/SEMrush

## 🎯 Step 2: Find Keyword Gaps You Can Target

### Method 1: Manual Spreadsheet Analysis (Free)

**Create This Spreadsheet:**

| Keyword | Your Rank | Competitor 1 | Competitor 2 | Search Volume | Difficulty | Opportunity |
|---------|-----------|--------------|--------------|---------------|------------|-------------|
| luxury accommodation taupo | 15 | 1 | 3 | 880 | Medium | HIGH |
| taupo holiday home spa | Not ranking | 5 | 8 | 320 | Low | HIGH |
| 5 bedroom taupo | 8 | 2 | 4 | 210 | Low | MEDIUM |

**Opportunity Score:**
- **HIGH** = High search volume + You don't rank + Low difficulty
- **MEDIUM** = Medium volume + You rank poorly (10+) + Medium difficulty  
- **LOW** = Low volume OR high difficulty

### Method 2: Keyword Gap Tools (Paid)

#### **Ahrefs Content Gap:**
```
1. Content Gap → Enter your domain
2. Add 3-5 competitor domains
3. Show keywords where: Competitors rank in top 10, you don't
4. Filter: KD (Keyword Difficulty) < 30
5. Filter: Traffic potential > 50
6. Export opportunities
```

#### **SEMrush Keyword Gap:**
```
1. Keyword Gap → Your domain vs competitors
2. Filter: "Missing" keywords
3. Filter: Volume > 100
4. Filter: KD% < 40
5. Create content targeting these gaps
```

## 🔗 Step 3: Analyze Competitor Backlink Profiles

### Why Backlinks Matter:
- Google's #1 ranking factor
- Build domain authority
- Drive referral traffic
- Show trustworthiness

### Free Methods:

#### **A. Manual Link Discovery**
Search Google for:
```
"taupo accommodation" -site:thepillarstaupo.com
"best places to stay taupo"
"taupo travel guide"
"things to do taupo"
```

Look for:
- Travel blogs mentioning competitors
- Tourism websites with listings
- Local business directories
- Press mentions

**Create Outreach List:**
Contact these sites and ask for:
- Directory listing
- Blog feature/mention
- Partnership opportunity

#### **B. Check Their Social Mentions**
Search on:
- Instagram: `#taupoaccommmodation`
- Facebook: Search "Taupo accommodation recommendations"
- TripAdvisor: See who's getting reviews
- Google Reviews: Competitor review profiles

### Paid Tools:

#### **A. Ahrefs Backlink Checker** (Best Option)
https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker

**Free Version:**
- 100 backlinks per domain
- Good starting point

**Paid Version ($99/mo):**
```
1. Site Explorer → Enter competitor
2. Backlinks → See ALL their links
3. Filter: DR (Domain Rating) > 30
4. Filter: Dofollow links only
5. Sort by: Referring domains
```

**What to Look For:**
- **High DR Links:** DR 50+ websites linking to them
- **Tourism Directories:** newzealand.com, bookabach.co.nz
- **Blog Features:** Travel bloggers who mentioned them
- **Local Listings:** Taupo tourism sites
- **Press Mentions:** News articles

**Replication Strategy:**
1. Export their best backlinks (DR 30+)
2. Visit each linking website
3. Email: "Hi, I noticed you featured [Competitor]. I run One Eleven Taupo, a luxury 5-bedroom lakefront property. Would you be interested in featuring us too?"

#### **B. Moz Link Explorer** (~$99/month)
https://moz.com/link-explorer

**Best Features:**
- Domain Authority (DA) score
- Spam score (avoid bad links)
- Link opportunities report

#### **C. SEMrush Backlink Analytics**
```
1. Backlink Analytics → Competitor domain
2. Backlinks → Export all
3. Filter: Authority Score > 30
4. Referring Domains → Find linking opportunities
5. Backlink Gap → See who links to competitors but not you
```
